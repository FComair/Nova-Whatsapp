# Data Processing Agreement (DPA)

## 1. Purpose and roles
This agreement supplements the Terms of Use. For the personal data of its own clients entered in Rendivoo (“Customer Data”), the business is the controller and {{company}} (“Rendivoo”) is the processor within the meaning of Article 28 GDPR.

## 2. Nature, purpose and duration
Rendivoo processes Customer Data only to provide the service (calendar, client database, WhatsApp messages, reports) and on the Customer’s documented instructions, for the duration of the contract and then until erasure.

## 3. Data subjects and data
Data subjects: the Customer’s clients and prospects. Data: identity and contact details (name, WhatsApp number, email), appointments, conversation history, preferences and consents, notes entered by the Customer. The Customer avoids entering unnecessary sensitive data.

## 4. Rendivoo’s obligations
Rendivoo processes data on the Customer’s instructions, ensures the confidentiality of its staff, applies the security measures in section 7, helps the Customer respond to data subject requests and meet its security, notification and impact-assessment obligations, and tells the Customer if an instruction seems to breach the law.

## 5. Sub-processors
The Customer authorises Rendivoo to use sub-processors needed for the service (hosting, email delivery, WhatsApp/Meta). Rendivoo imposes equivalent obligations on them, remains liable for their performance and informs the Customer of any change of sub-processor, to which the Customer may object on reasonable grounds.

## 6. Transfers outside the European Economic Area
Transfers outside the European Economic Area are made only with appropriate safeguards (for example standard contractual clauses).

## 7. Security
Isolation of data between businesses, encryption of exchanges and sensitive data, role-based access control, two-factor authentication for sensitive roles, append-only audit logs, backups.

## 8. Personal data breach
Rendivoo notifies the Customer without undue delay after becoming aware of a breach affecting Customer Data, with the information needed for the Customer to notify it.

## 9. Data subject rights
Rendivoo forwards to the Customer any request received directly from a data subject and provides the service’s export and erasure tools to help the Customer respond.

## 10. Audit
Rendivoo makes available the information needed to demonstrate compliance with this agreement and allows reasonable audits, announced in advance and without disrupting other customers.

## 11. End of processing
At the end of the contract or on request, Rendivoo deletes Customer Data according to the service’s deletion schedule, after the Customer has been able to export it, unless the law requires retention.

## 12. Liability and governing law
The parties’ liability is as set out in the Terms of Use. This agreement is governed by Belgian law.
