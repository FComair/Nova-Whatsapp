# Privacy Policy

## 1. Who is responsible
{{company}} is the controller of the data of your Nova WhatsApp account (users of the customer area). For the data of your own clients that you enter in Nova, you are the controller and Nova acts as processor under the Data Processing Agreement (DPA).

## 2. Data we collect
- **Account**: email address, password (hashed), business phone, language, business sector, role in the business.
- **Use**: actions carried out in the service (audit log), IP address and browser at sign-in and acceptance.
- **Billing**: billing identity, invoices and payments; no card data is kept by Nova.
- **Support**: your messages to support and their attachments.

## 3. Why and on what basis
To provide the service and manage the account (performance of the contract); to invoice and meet our accounting and legal obligations (legal obligation); to secure the service and prevent fraud (legitimate interest); to send optional communications only with your consent.

## 4. Recipients
We share data with providers needed for the service: hosting, email delivery, payment, and WhatsApp (Meta) for sending messages. They act on our instructions and under contractual safeguards. We do not sell your data.

## 5. Transfers outside the European Economic Area
Where a provider processes data outside the European Economic Area, we ensure appropriate safeguards (for example standard contractual clauses).

## 6. Retention
Account data is kept as long as the account exists. When the account is deleted, it is erased after thirty days, except invoices and payments kept for seven years (accounting obligation), whose identity is then pseudonymised. The log of legal acceptances is kept to prove the consent given.

## 7. Your rights
You can request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability of your data from Settings or by contacting us. You can lodge a complaint with the data protection authority of your country.

## 8. Security
We apply technical and organisational measures: encrypted exchanges, isolation of data between businesses, audit logs, two-factor authentication for sensitive roles.

## 9. Cookies
We only use strictly necessary cookies (session, security, language). They are not used for advertising or tracking.

## 10. Changes
We may update this policy; each version is dated and kept. For an important change we ask you to confirm that you have read it.

## 11. Contact
For any question or to exercise your rights, use Nova’s Help area or write to {{company}}.
