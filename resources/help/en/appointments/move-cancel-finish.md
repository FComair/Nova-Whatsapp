Click an appointment in **Schedule** to open its details.

## Move

**Move** lets you change the date, time and practitioner. Tick **Notify the customer on WhatsApp** to send them the new slot.

## Cancel

**Cancel** asks for an optional reason. The customer is notified if the box is ticked.

## Finish

Once the time has passed, a confirmed appointment can be marked **Completed** or **No-show** (the customer did not come). These results feed the figures on the customer’s profile.

## Payment

**Payment** records the status (unpaid, paid on site, partially paid…), the amount collected and the method. Nova does not process payments: it only notes them.

> **If the customer cannot be notified** (they replied STOP or cannot be reached), the action is still done and Nova asks you to contact them directly.
