In **Schedule**, click **New appointment**.

## The steps

1. Choose the **service**, then the **practitioner**: only those who perform that service are listed.
2. Pick the **date**, then the **hour** and **minutes** from the lists.
3. Enter the customer’s **phone** with the country code (for example +32 470 12 34 56) and their **name**. The customer is found by number, or created automatically.
4. Add **notes** if needed (they are hidden from receptionists).
5. Leave **Notify the customer on WhatsApp** ticked so they receive the confirmation.

## If the slot is not free

Nova refuses a slot outside opening hours or already taken, and suggests the next three free slots for the same practitioner. A blocked customer cannot be given appointments.

> **Good to know:** as staff you are not limited by the minimum notice or the booking horizon: only opening hours and double bookings count.
