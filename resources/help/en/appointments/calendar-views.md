At the top of the **Schedule**, the view switcher offers four layouts. On a phone it is a drop-down list.

## The four views

- **Day**: a single day in detail, with the practitioner and status on each appointment.
- **3 days**: the chosen day and the next two, even across two weeks.
- **Working week**: Monday to Sunday, hiding the days you are closed.
- **Practitioners**: one column per practitioner for the chosen day. Pick a single practitioner to see their week.

## Navigating

The arrows move by one day, 3 days or one week depending on the view. The **Today** (or **This week**) button takes you back to the present. The date and location stay the same when you change view.

## The hours

The grid starts two hours before opening and ends two hours after closing. Hours outside opening are hatched; opening hours are white.
