The QR code is reserved for the **owner**: **Settings > WhatsApp QR code**.

## How it works

1. Create a QR code. A customer scans it with their phone: WhatsApp opens a conversation with your number.
2. They write, and Rendivoo’s assistant helps them book.
3. Download the QR code to print on a poster, a counter, a business card or a shop window.

## See what it brings

Customers who arrive this way are recorded with the source **WhatsApp QR code**: find them with the **Source** filter in the **Customers** list.

> **Prerequisite:** your WhatsApp number must be connected (see “Connect your WhatsApp number”).
