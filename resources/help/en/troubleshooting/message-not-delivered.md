When Nova cannot notify a customer, the action (create, move, cancel) is still done and a message asks you to contact them directly. Here is what to check.

## Possible causes

1. **The customer replied STOP.** They no longer receive any automatic message. See “What happens when a customer replies STOP?”.
2. **The number is wrong**, or the customer has no WhatsApp on that number. Check the country code on their profile.
3. **WhatsApp is not connected.** Check the status in **Settings > WhatsApp**.
4. **A message template is not approved** in the customer’s language: writing outside the 24-hour window requires templates approved by WhatsApp.
5. **“Notify the customer on WhatsApp” was unticked** when the action was made.

## Next

Contact the customer directly by phone. If none of these causes applies, note the time and the customer concerned before contacting support.
