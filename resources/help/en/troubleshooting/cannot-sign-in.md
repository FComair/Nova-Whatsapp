## Forgotten password

On the sign-in page, use **Forgot password**: Rendivoo sends a reset link to your email address. Choose a password of at least 12 characters.

## The two-step code is rejected

- Check that your **phone’s time** is set automatically: a few minutes off is enough to invalidate the code.
- Wait for the app’s next code and type it without spaces.
- If you lost your phone, use a **recovery code** (each works once).

## Too many attempts

After several wrong codes, sign-in is blocked for a few minutes. Wait, then try again without repeated attempts.

## Email address not verified

Open the confirmation email you received at sign-up, or ask for a new one from the verification page.
