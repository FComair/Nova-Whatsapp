Everything is in **Settings > Security**. For each change, Nova asks for your **current password**.

## Password

At least 12 characters, with an upper-case letter, a lower-case letter, a digit and a special character. Your last 5 passwords cannot be reused. Changing your password closes your other sessions.

## Email address

Enter the new address: Nova sends a confirmation link to it and informs the old one. The change only happens after confirmation.

## Business phone

Enter the new number with its country code (+32…). A code is sent to the **new** number; the old one stays active until the code is confirmed.

## Sessions

You can close your other sessions if you think a device is no longer safe.
