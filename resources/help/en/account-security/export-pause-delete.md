These actions are in **Settings > My data** and reserved for the **owner**.

## Export your data

Download a copy of your business’s data: customers, appointments, services, team. Useful for your records or a portability request.

## Pause

Pausing suspends the business temporarily (holiday, closure). The affected appointments are cancelled and customers are notified; you can resume afterwards.

## Delete the account

Deletion asks for a **two-step confirmation**. The business’s data is then erased, except what the law requires to keep (invoices). Remember to **export your data first**.

> **Only want to erase one customer?** See “Erase a customer’s data”.
