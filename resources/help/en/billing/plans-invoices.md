The **Billing** page is reserved for the **owner**. It stays reachable even if your account is suspended, so you can put things right.

## What you do there

- See your **plan** and change it.
- View and download your **invoices**.
- Add or change your **payment method**.

## During the trial

The days left appear on the **Dashboard**. Choose a plan before it ends to carry on without interruption.

## If a payment fails

Rendivoo warns you and tries again. Update your payment method in **Billing** to avoid your account being suspended.
