In the **Customers** list, tick one or more customers: an action bar appears.

## Tags

**Add a tag** or **Remove a tag** applies to the whole selection (20 tags at most per customer). Tags feed search and filters. They are open to the owner, site managers and receptionists.

## Archive or block

- **Archive** removes customers from the default list without touching their history; **Restore** puts them back. Archiving does not stop them from booking. Reserved for the owner and site managers.
- **Block** (on the customer’s profile) prevents any new appointment.

None of these actions erases data. To erase a customer’s personal data, see “Erase a customer’s data”.
