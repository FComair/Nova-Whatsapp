A customer can ask for their data to be erased. Open their profile from **Customers**: the action is reserved for the owner and site managers.

## What is deleted

Their name, email, language, tags, profile details, WhatsApp conversations and appointment notes. Their number is replaced by an unusable dummy number.

## What is kept

Appointment history (dates, services, prices) is kept **with no link to a person**, for your statistics and accounting.

## Before erasing

Erasure is refused while the customer has upcoming appointments: cancel them first. The operation is **final**.
