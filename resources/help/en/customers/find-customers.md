The **Customers** page lists your customers with their last and next appointment, visits and value.

## Search

A single search box that starts at 2 characters. It ignores accents and capital letters and finds name, email, reference (for example C-1042) and tags. A number is found **whatever its format**: 0470 12 34 56, +32 470 12 34 56 or 0032470123456.

## Filters

The **Filters** button offers status, appointments (upcoming, never visited, no recent visit, with a no-show), WhatsApp consent, practitioner, tag, source, new customers and a minimum number of visits.

## Views

Ready-made views (upcoming appointment, no visit for 90 days, new, with a no-show, refused messages) are provided. **Save this view** keeps your filters and columns; the owner and site managers can share it with the team. The **Columns** button chooses what is shown.

> **Value** is computed from the amounts collected (or prices) of completed appointments. Rendivoo does not process payments.
