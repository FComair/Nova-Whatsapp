There are two levels of hours, both in **Settings**.

- **Location opening hours**: in **Business basics**. They say when your business is open.
- **Practitioner working hours**: in **Services & team**, for each practitioner and location. They say when they can see customers.

## Entering hours

Hours and minutes are chosen from lists. The start time must be before the end time. You can add several ranges on the same day (for example a lunch break).

## In the schedule

Opening and working hours are white, the rest is hatched. The **Working week** view hides days when the location is closed; **Day** and **3 days** still show them.

A slot is only offered to customers within the working hours of a practitioner who performs the service.
