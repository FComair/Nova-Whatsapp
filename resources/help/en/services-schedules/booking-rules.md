These rules apply when your **customers** book, move or cancel. Set them in **Settings > Services & team**.

## The three rules

- **Minimum notice before an appointment (hours)**: a customer cannot book later than this before the appointment time. 0 = immediate booking allowed.
- **Bookable up to (days ahead)**: beyond this, no slots are offered.
- **Cancellation or rescheduling allowed until (hours before)**: after that, the customer must contact you. 0 = until the last moment.

The minimum notice must stay shorter than the booking period.

## For staff

When you create or move an appointment from the schedule, these rules do not block you: only opening hours and double bookings count.
