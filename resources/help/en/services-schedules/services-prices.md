Services are managed in **Settings > Services & team** (owner).

## Add a service

Enter its name, duration and price. Then link each service to the **practitioners** who perform it: a practitioner only appears in an appointment for the services linked to them.

## Import a price list

Instead of typing everything, you can import a photo or screenshot of your price list. Rendivoo reads the image, shows the lines it found and lets you tick the ones to add or update. Always check the prices before confirming.

## Fix a mistake

An import can be **undone** afterwards: the services added or changed go back to how they were.

> **Tip:** if no slot is offered for a service, check that at least one active practitioner performs it.
