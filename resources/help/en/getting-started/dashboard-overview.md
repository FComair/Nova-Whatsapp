The **Dashboard** is the first item in the menu. It sums up the day.

## What you will find

- **The figures**: appointments today, still to come today, cancelled today, tomorrow, the next 7 days and new customers in the last 7 days.
- **Next appointment** and the list of today’s appointments with their status. **Open the schedule** takes you straight to the grid.
- **First steps**: the getting-started actions, struck through once done.
- During the trial, the number of days left.

The figures follow your role: a practitioner only sees their own.
