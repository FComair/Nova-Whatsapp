The first time you sign in, the setup assistant guides you. You can stop with **Continue later** and pick it up again at any time from **Settings**.

## The four parts of the setup

1. **Business basics**: your business (name, sector, country, time zone), your locations and your opening hours.
2. **Services & team**: your services (duration and price), your practitioners with their working hours, and your booking rules.
3. **Customer messages**: connecting your WhatsApp number and the message templates that WhatsApp must approve.
4. **Go live & security**: a test booking and protecting your account (two-step verification).

## Follow your progress

On the **Dashboard**, the “First steps” list shows what is left to do. In **Settings**, each part shows how many steps remain.

> **Tip:** start with opening hours and services. Without them, no slot is offered to your customers.
