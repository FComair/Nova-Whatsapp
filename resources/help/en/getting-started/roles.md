Everyone on the team has a role. It decides what is visible and what they can do.

## The four roles

- **Owner**: everything, including setup, WhatsApp, billing, data export and account deletion.
- **Site manager**: the schedule and customers of their locations, erasing a customer’s data, archiving customers.
- **Receptionist**: the schedule and customers of their locations, creating and editing appointments, tags. Appointment notes are hidden from them. No archiving or erasing.
- **Practitioner**: only their own appointments and the customers they have served. They cannot change an appointment’s practitioner or act on several customers at once.

## Good to know

The **Settings** menu only shows the pages your role gives access to. Two-step verification is mandatory for the owner and site managers.
